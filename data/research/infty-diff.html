∞-Diff: infinite resolution diffusion

This research uses diffusion models to generate images at any resolution while retaining sharp detail. ∞-Diff, accepted at ICLR 2024, models images as continuous functions and learns by denoising randomly sampled coordinates, reducing how much of each image needs to be processed:

Neural operators share information between coordinates without first compressing the image into a latent vector. Our multiscale architecture combines local processing of sparse points with a regular grid for global context. A mollified diffusion process smooths its states, making them easier to model with continuous operators. The same model can then sample beyond its training resolution:

In experiments, training with up to eightfold coordinate subsampling retained good sample quality while reducing runtime and memory use. This makes high-resolution diffusion more practical while preserving the ability to choose the sampling resolution. See the paper and source code for the architecture and experiments.
